The mission

Too many hikers turn back or risk a fall because they don’t have traction on ice and snow. Free Spikes exists to change that.

We carry microspikes on our own hikes and offer a free pair when we see someone struggling on steep or icy terrain. Recipients can keep them for future trips or pass them on to another hiker who needs them.

Each pair comes with a small pouch or bag printed with a link back to this site — so people can learn more, share their story, or help keep the project going.

Goal: cover costs while increasing safety and outdoor experiences and support the parks we enjoy.

How it works

1

We hike

While out on trails, we watch for hikers who look like they’re having trouble with ice or steep slopes.

2

We offer

If someone needs traction, we offer a free pair of microspikes. No pressure, no charge. “How much?” → “They’re free. Here’s the website if you want to learn more or help.”

3

You decide

Keep them for future hikes or pass them forward to someone else who needs traction. Visit the link on the bag to share your story or support the project.

About the gear

These are microspikes (not full mountaineering crampons) — lightweight, easy to put on over regular boots, and perfect for most icy trail conditions. Multiple sizes, each with a printed pouch/bag that includes a link to this site.

FAQ

Are these full crampons?

No — they’re microspikes. Lighter, simpler, and ideal for most icy trail conditions rather than steep technical ice climbing.

Where are you distributing?

Distribution is opportunistic while hiking. We offer pairs when we see someone who needs them on the trail.

Can I request a pair?

Right now distribution is opportunistic while hiking. If you’re organizing a group hike in an area we’ll be, reach out and we may be able to help.

What if I don’t need them anymore?

Keep them for future hikes or pass them on to another hiker who needs traction. The goal is maximum usefulness.

How are donations used?

Donations reimburse the cost of the microspikes and project expenses. Proceeds are donated to parks and recreation sites we enjoy.
